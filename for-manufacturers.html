For manufacturers and suppliers

List your products in the Construction R-value Calculator

New Zealand designers use the calculator to work out H1/AS1 6th edition construction R-values for walls, roofs and floors — and they pick the insulation and cladding there, layer by layer. A listing puts your products in that list, with your declared values and your data source, at the moment the product is chosen.

What a listing includes

  • Your products in the picker — insulation in the product list beside each framing layer (walls, ceilings, skillion roofs, floors); claddings under your brand in the cladding dropdowns.
  • Your declared values — product name, R-value, thickness and width, used directly in the calculation.
  • Your source, shown under the list — the data sheet, BRANZ Appraisal or CodeMark certificate your values come from.
  • Named in the designer's PDF — the calculation report that goes with the building consent names the product used in each layer.

The rules every listing follows

The calculator is used for building consent, so designers need to trust that its numbers are not for sale. These rules are what make a listing worth having:

  1. A listing never changes the calculation. The method is NZS 4214 as modified by H1/AS1 2.1.3.2 and the Appendix E tables, for every product alike. The code is open source, so anyone can check.
  2. Payment does not buy position. Insulation is listed by R-value and thickness, whoever makes it.
  3. Generic options stay. Designers can always choose a generic material or type their own R-value.
  4. Same terms for everyone. Every manufacturer is offered the same listing, at the same price.
  5. Published, citable values only. Values must come from your own published data, with a source designers can check. You are responsible for their accuracy; values that can't be verified are not listed, and a listing can be removed if they turn out to be wrong.
  6. Corrections are always free. If a product of yours is already in the calculator and its data needs updating, tell us — there is no charge.

How to get listed

Send us your product range: names, declared R-values, thicknesses and widths (for insulation) or thicknesses and conductivity (for claddings), and the document they come from. We'll reply with the terms and a preview of how your products will appear.
